# Clip di apertura Reel — prompt Higgsfield

Impostazioni: image-to-video, 9:16, 5 s, start frame `frame-iniziale-9x16.jpg`
(oppure `foglio-dritto.jpg` se preferisci far ritagliare a Higgsfield).
Movimento camera, se usi i preset: Dolly In / Push In lento.

## Prompt

Top-down overhead shot of a handwritten sheet of white paper that fills the
frame, lying on a beige tablecloth with a brown floral pattern. The camera slowly and smoothly
pushes in toward the small handwritten word "MEGLIO" in blue ballpoint ink,
until it sits in the centre of the frame. As the camera arrives, a thin
hand-drawn line in the same blue ballpoint ink starts under the word "MEGLIO"
and extends steadily to the right until it crosses the whole frame and exits
the right edge. Keep the original handwriting exactly as in the reference
image: same letters, same strokes, no new words. Soft natural daylight from a
window, gentle shadows, realistic paper texture, shallow depth of field at the
end of the move. Calm, steady motion, no camera shake. Keep the upper third and
lower third of the frame clean and uncluttered.

## Negative prompt

added text, captions, subtitles, titles, watermark, logo, changed or redrawn
handwriting, extra words, misspelled letters, hands, pen, people, harsh light,
flash, fast zoom, camera shake, warping paper, morphing letters
